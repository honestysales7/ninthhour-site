Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Ninth Hour: Prayer & Devotion ("Ninth Hour", "the app") is made by Honesty Sales Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 15075596), PA103 Technology Centre, Glaisher Drive, Wolverhampton WV10 9RU, United Kingdom. Contact: apps@honestysales.com.

We built Ninth Hour to help you pray and read God's Word. We collect as little as possible.

What stays on your phone

  • Your location. Ninth Hour uses your approximate location, or the town you type, only to work out sunrise and sunset so your prayer times follow the sun. It is saved on your phone and is not sent to us. To show the name of your town, your phone's own location service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone) may look up the place name.
  • Your prayer record, reading and journal. Prayers you complete, chapters you read and reflections you write are stored only on your phone. We cannot see them.
  • Your settings, such as your prayer plan, call sounds and times.

Calls to prayer are scheduled by your phone itself. We do not run a server that sends you messages.

Subscriptions

Ninth Hour Premium is sold through Google Play (and the App Store on iPhone). The store takes your payment; we never see your card or bank details.

We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to manage subscriptions. When you open the app, RevenueCat receives a random app user ID created by the app, your device type and app version, and the record of any Ninth Hour purchase from the store, so it can tell the app whether Premium is active. It does not receive your name, email, location or journal. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad trackers.
  • No analytics or tracking of what you read or pray.
  • We never sell your data.

Legal basis and your rights

We process subscription records to provide the service you ask for (performance of a contract) and to meet legal and accounting duties. Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email apps@honestysales.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Ninth Hour removes everything stored on your phone. To delete the subscription record held by RevenueCat, email apps@honestysales.com and we will remove it. Purchase records kept by Google or Apple are covered by their own policies.

Children

Ninth Hour is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above.
